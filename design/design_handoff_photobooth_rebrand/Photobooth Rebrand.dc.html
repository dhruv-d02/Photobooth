naming concepts

three ownable, mallcore-flavored names — not descriptive labels. pick one and it carries through every screen below.

top pick
boothie
a nickname, not a gadget. sounds like something you'd call your camera roll's main character. mascot-ready, easy to say, easy to love.
4EVER
Y2K numeral-speak — the way you'd actually text it. leans hardest into nostalgia: "besties 4ever" energy, straight off the back of a real strip.
picnic
a real word, repurposed — pic + nic. warmer and less gadget-y, more "hangout with friends" than "camera app."
pick one (or mix & match) — the phone screens below use the "appName" tweak so you can preview any of the three live ✧
